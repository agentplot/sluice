# sluice rules

The rules that bind wherever a change is made in this repository,
written so a reviewer can point at a diff and say which rule it breaks.
The narrative is the statement in agentplot/blueprints,
`design/sluice/requirements.md`; a rule here names the clause it
enforces and never restates it. There are no area pages yet; a rule
that binds only one part of the tree moves to an area page when that
part exists.

## Rules

### The core and its seams

- **[core.1]** Code outside a profile names no task runner, worktree tool, multiplexer, service manager or forge.
- **[core.2]** Every seam of Part B is reached through exactly one interface, and a profile implements that interface and nothing of the core.
- **[core.3]** A seam with a single profile has an ADR saying why one is enough.

### Branches and landings

- **[core.4]** No code path reachable from the submit operation writes a branch's ref; a round of sluice's work may be run by any process, a waiting driver included.
- **[core.5]** A branch is moved only with the expected old revision, and a move whose expectation fails is reported, never retried blind.
- **[core.6]** A candidate is formed from the branch's revision and the entries ahead of it, in order, and from nothing else.
- **[core.17]** A candidate whose entries ahead land exactly as they were tested is the same candidate on the revision they land as: its tree, its runs and its verdict stand, and it is named as that revision extended by the entries still ahead of it.
- **[core.39]** A submission whose work no required check reads, by the check's affected reading at the branch's current revision and at each candidate formed and not landed, as it would land now, and which merges cleanly onto them and onto the work of every entry ahead, lands at the first round that reads it, beside the chain and with nothing run; a candidate formed before it keeps its runs and verdict and lands from the revision it made, carrying its work, never formed or tested again for it.
- **[core.27]** Which remote a branch is on is read from the policy at every revision of the branch the host holds, its own ref and each remote's tracking ref, never from the host's own ref alone and never by a fetch, and from each revision only as far as that reading needs, the remote named for the branch and whether signers are named, so a revision this sluice refuses elsewhere in its policy decides the remote all the same.
- **[core.28]** A read of a remote the core could not make is logged on the branch with the remote's own words, once per distinct cause and host, with its end, and is never reported only to the process that met it.
- **[core.29]** Every call to a remote runs under sluice's one retry policy, a maintained crate's backoff bounded by its caller, which tries again what the remote never judged, a credential it refused bought afresh for the next try, never what the remote answered, and never a push or a forge write whose answer was not read, which is settled by reading the branch or the forge, a write nothing reads back reported unanswered; and a forge's word that an allowance is spent is a pause until the time it names, kept in the host's state and honoured by every process on the host, which a command waits out within its own limit and a round passes by.
- **[core.31]** Nothing of sluice's need run between its commands for any guarantee to hold, and no policy, configuration or document of sluice's tells a host to run a daemon; where a host runs `sluice run`, it is the host's reader and a wait or a watch that finds it live runs no round; a submission's outcome is pushed into its submitter's session as a notice and marked delivered by sluice alone, on the word of the carrier that delivered it and never on a notify that delivered nothing, no carrier keeping a list of its own of what it delivered; and a session that starts collects every outcome pending for its own submissions by `sluice inbox`, so no wait need stand for an outcome to arrive; and a reader's tick asks the forge nothing but what the discovery due or a move on the host calls for.

### Forge reads

- **[core.32]** sluice asks the forge only what could have moved: the open requests at the deployment's interval or after a move on the host, every read conditional on the answer the host keeps for its address, a request's reviews, checks, ready marks and comments only once it or its entry moved or while it waits on its conditions, a draft by the list alone, one query per branch where the forge answers one, and its own check and report posted only when what they say changes.
- **[core.33]** A branch's requests are taken in by one host at a time: the instance that keeps the branch where its records are on its remote, otherwise the holder of the branch's intake mark there, which a driver pushes when it starts and renews never each pass, or where none stands the host there alone; a request's entry is advanced only on that host, and a wait elsewhere takes nothing in and follows its request on the forge.

### Verdicts and attribution

- **[core.7]** Every check run is recorded with its key, outcome and executor before any verdict is read from it, a fail exactly as a pass.
- **[core.8]** A failure is attributed to one entry, and only after every candidate ahead of it has a verdict.
- **[core.9]** A replay is recorded as a replay, never as a run.
- **[core.22]** A result counts for a gate only through a signer the branch's policy names and a claim that signer may make, never through what a record says of its executor alone; on a branch in one host's own files, through the runs that host's sluice starts.
- **[core.34]** A check's results are weighed together only under one grant: a key's outcomes for a flake, and the pass that proves a replay, are read only from results whose executor gave the run's own grant, on every branch, signed or not, and a result recorded with no grant only with others that have none.
- **[core.35]** A check that did not pass in a run whose executor reports refusing the run something has no outcome; the refusal is read from the executor's own report, never from what a check printed or how it failed, and a check that passed keeps its pass.
- **[core.37]** sluice runs no readiness command the repository writes, before a run or after one: a run has no outcome only on the executor's own report of a refusal, the check's own not-run exit, or the executor's own finding after a run in which a check did not pass that a requirement the policy asks has left the host, never on a probe sluice or the repository runs.
- **[core.38]** A run whose policy asks for a container engine is claimed only by an executor that finds one the run can reach; one that finds none refuses the run before claiming it and leaves it open for a host that can; after a run in which a check did not pass the executor looks at the engine again, and where it has gone the run has no outcome, nobody is blamed and a pass stands; a requirement is read from the host only where the executor can see it for itself and a check cannot bring it up.

### Places

- **[core.10]** The core starts no service; the checks it runs are one process group, ended with their place or their time limit, and what they hand to another process is removed when their place is released.
- **[core.11]** Every record a reader may need is readable with no sluice process running, from every host that submits to its branch.
- **[core.14]** Nothing the core decides is held between steps outside the records, and no two steps act on one branch at once.
- **[core.15]** A branch's gate is read from the branch's current revision, never from a candidate or a place.
- **[core.16]** The core releases only places it made, and a session's only when its working copy is gone; otherwise a session's place is released only by `sluice release` at its session's or driver's asking, and a preview only by `sluice release` at its caller's asking or when its command failed or its checkout is gone.
- **[core.18]** A session run counts toward the bound from the host's state and is recorded under its keys only when its worktree holds exactly its head.
- **[core.19]** A preview is made only at a revision its branch has landed, and decides nothing: its command is never recorded as a run, read by a gate, or waited on by a submission.
- **[core.20]** The core writes no working copy it neither made nor adopted: a worktree that has checked out a branch sluice lands on is reported, never moved, and never stops a landing.
- **[core.23]** An instance claims no run while a place it made belongs to a candidate with a verdict, an obsolete one, or a claim it no longer holds, until each such place is released or held.
- **[core.30]** A place's scratch is at a path that does not grow with the place's name or its checkout's location, and the place's record names it.

### Design and records

- **[core.12]** A change that bears on a clause cites the clause by number; a clause that no longer holds is changed in the statement first, never worked around here.
- **[core.13]** Nothing commercial, no pricing, tier or customer, appears in this repository.
- **[core.26]** The site, the README, the plugins' skills and the commands' output speak to any host's operator: no word the statement coined that the reader cannot see in the product, no machine, fleet, partition, deployment or tool of ours, and no team's branch scheme as sluice's own; a skill that proposes a branch or a pattern follows the project's own naming.
- **[core.25]** On a host, at most one process reads a repository's records to watch its queues; every other watch and wait follows that reader's document.

### The gate

- **[core.21]** Every file the tree tracks is owned by a formatter the gate runs, or is excluded by name with its reason; every language with a linter is linted; every directory under `plugins/`, and `site/`, is a moon project of its own, listed in `.moon/workspace.yml` with its language declared, whose check-fast tasks validate, type-check and test it, one task per check.
- **[core.24]** Every tool a gate task runs is the version the tree pins, on every host that runs the gate, so CI and `sluice exec` hold a tree to the same rules.

## Details

- **core.1** Rules out: the strings `moon`, `wt`, `worktrunk`, `devenv`, `herdr`, `gh`, `crew`, `flywheel` in any module outside a profile directory; an import of a profile from the core. Not ruled out: `git`, which section 9 of the statement fixes; `plugins/`, which no crate imports. Source: statement preamble and clause 52; ADRs 0003, 0023.
- **core.2** Rules out: a profile calling another profile; a core function with a profile-specific branch. Source: clauses 52, 53; ADR 0003.
- **core.3** Rules out: a seam whose only profile is undocumented as such. Source: ADR 0003.
- **core.4** Rules out: `update-ref`, `push`, a merge or a fast-forward in the submit operation or anything it calls; a round that behaves differently because a driver ran it; a submit pushing, or writing the host's copy of, a branch sluice lands on (124). Not ruled out: a waiting `sluice submit`, `wt submit` or `sluice wait` running rounds between reads of its submission's state; a pull request's submit pushing the session's own branch, which sluice lands on nowhere (124). Source: clauses 1, 124; ADRs 0002, 0005, 0011, 0041, 0053.
- **core.5** Rules out: `update-ref` without an old value; a landing loop that re-reads and retries on refusal without re-forming the chain. Not ruled out: a leased push the remote refused on sluice's credential before any ref was negotiated, tried again under the retry policy on a credential bought afresh, with the lease and the expectation of the first, since the remote it refused is as it was (134; record 0062). Source: clauses 24, 45, 46, 134.
- **core.6** Rules out: a candidate formed from a cached revision of the branch; a candidate including an entry removed as culprit or conflict, or an entry behind it; a candidate formed on a revision that lacks the branch's last landing (121). Not ruled out: a candidate formed before work landed beside the chain landing with that work in its tree (140, core.39; record 0075). Source: clauses 8, 10, 12, 21, 121; ADR 0051.
- **core.17** Rules out: testing a candidate again because the entries ahead of it landed as tested; telling a culprit, or recording, a combination by a revision the branch has left when the branch has since landed the entries ahead of it. Holds because a landing commit carries the tested candidate's tree (23): the branch after the landing and the candidate before it hold the same tree. Not ruled out: such a candidate landing from a revision that landings beside the chain alone moved since it was formed, its tree the tested tree with their work, its runs and verdict standing, since that work no required check reads (24, 140, core.39; record 0075). Source: clauses 10, 20, 23; answers to the reconstruction, 12; ADR 0006.
- **core.39** Rules out: a submission whose work no required check reads waiting behind a candidate under test or held to land (2026-10-30: a docs-only record of another repository's on the host tested in one combination with code submissions 399 and 400 and waiting out their checks); a candidate formed or tested again because work landed beside it; work landed beside the chain that a formed candidate's checks would read, asked of the branch's revision alone; work landed beside that does not merge cleanly with an entry ahead, making an earlier submission conflict; the branch's policy, or what the check's or the place's profile names as its own setting, landed beside; a landing beside on a revision no verdict covers (70); path patterns or a policy field standing for the affected reading; a mixed submission split, its docs landed beside; a head of an act landed beside before a head it is after; a signer asked for a landing that reads no run; a landing beside made by a host that neither advances the branch nor, for a request, takes its requests in. Not ruled out: priority ordering no landing beside; the script profile, whose affected reading is every check, qualifying nothing; work a candidate's checks would read, or that conflicts with an entry ahead, taking the chain and saying which entry it waits behind; the affected reading asked once per formed candidate; a leased push refused and reported, the entry tried again next round. Source: clauses 6, 8, 10, 13, 15, 23, 24, 36, 56, 58, 70, 78, 125, 137, 140, S68 (added or amended 2026-10-30); records 0044, 0075.
- **core.27** Rules out: a routing that reads a branch as local because the host's own ref holds no policy, or one naming no remote, while a remote's tracking ref of the branch names one (deneb, 2026-10-26: agentplot-kit's and site-theme's `refs/heads/main` from before the policy file, `sluice branch create` refusing crew's bolt, signal 2026-10-26-madswan-planner-ec418792/01); a fetch of any remote run to decide the routing; a branch routed to a remote no revision names; two revisions naming different remotes resolved by order rather than refused, naming both; a routing refused by a table or field of a revision's policy that names neither the remote nor a signer (deneb, 2026-10-28: `[[checkout.cache]]` at 4f6fe55f on the host's own ref of bolt/a-run-compiles-through-a-shared-cache refusing every read of the branch after the user's clean push to its remote, signal 2026-10-28-slc-2-conductor-3f0e396d/01; record 0069). Not ruled out: the fetch the pushed refs profile makes once the branch is routed to its remote (C.7); a host that holds no revision of the branch reading it as absent; the policy read whole at the branch's revision on its remote, for its gate (56). Source: clause 124 (as amended 2026-10-28); ADRs 0060, 0069.
- **core.28** Rules out: a round that fails on a refused fetch of the branch or its records with nothing written to the branch's log once it can write; a wait's `could not read` line as the one record of a standing refusal (deneb, 2026-10-26: swancloud-ops read `main/log.jsonl` for half an hour while every round's fetch was refused and found no fetch error, signal 2026-10-26-madswan-planner-c13058ca/01); a line per refused round; the remote's words shortened or rewritten; a refusal logged with no host. Not ruled out: the refusal's first time, words and count kept in the host's own state until the records on the remote can be written (73); the chore's second read on a fresh credential before the refusal is read as standing. Source: clause 133; ADR 0061.
- **core.29** Rules out: a sleep-and-try-again loop written at a call site (the wait's run through 5xx and the chore's second read, once the policy is in); a backoff, jitter or retry predicate of sluice's own in place of the crate's; a call to a remote, by git or by HTTP, made outside the policy; a push `[rejected]` under its lease, a protected branch or a 4xx that is the answer tried again; a push whose answer was not read tried again; a check run created, a comment posted or a workflow dispatched again after a server error without the forge read first; a workflow dispatch sent twice; a second try on the credential just refused; a retry logged on the branch per try. Not ruled out: the policy bounded to a wait's five minutes and to seconds for a round or a command; a fresh token settled before it is handed over; a landing left unfinished settled by the next step from its intent (122, `perform_landing`); `release_retries`, a release run once a round under the branch's policy; a request's draft mark set again, since the state read decides; a check run or a comment the forge already holds carried on; a dispatch whose connection was never made sent again; the crate's choice within the class of maintained backoff crates (backon recommended, 2026-10-27). Also rules out: a 403 or a 429 whose words or headers say a limit is spent, an answer leaving `x-ratelimit-remaining` at 0 or a GraphQL `RATE_LIMITED` read as the answer, or tried again by the backoff before the time GitHub named (installation 171576254, 2026-10-28: refused about fifteen minutes an hour, a submit or a wait failing on the 403); a pause held only in one process's memory; a call made to find out whether a recorded pause has ended; a submit, a wait or a status failing on a spent allowance while its own limit stands; a round that waits a pause out instead of passing the forge's part by. Not ruled out: a minute, then longer after each in a row, where GitHub names no time (its documentation's ask); a pause per installation and per allowance, REST's apart from GraphQL's; the other host learning the allowance is spent by its own first refused call. Source: clauses 45, 46, 133, 134 (as amended 2026-10-27 for forge writes, on batch 35's question, and 2026-10-28 for a spent allowance); ADRs 0017, 0061, 0062, 0071.
- **core.31** Rules out: a README, guide, skill or setup step that tells an adopter to run or install a service for sluice to work; a policy word or a host configuration key that requires a driver; a `sluice wait`, a waiting submit or a watch running rounds on a host where `sluice run` lives; an outcome delivered only by the wait that happened to read it; an outcome whose delivery is recorded nowhere; an entry marked delivered by a notify that delivered nothing (the worktrunk driver on main, 2026-10-28); a plugin's own list of outcomes delivered, or a plugin that marks sluice's records except through a sluice command; two carriers delivering one outcome to one session; a session restarted after its submission was decided left to find out by asking status; a reader's tick, a watch's or `sluice run`'s, that starts a process or reads an object while nothing in the repository changed (mac-studio, 2026-10-28, sluice 0.51.3: an idle `sluice run` in flywheel-web started git about 1364 times and made about 54,000 file events in ten minutes with nothing queued, reading each branch's policy through git every three-second pass, swancloud-ops's reading); a branch's policy read again while its revision stood. Not ruled out: a wait that runs rounds on a host where nothing else does, as today and for every plain install; a session that chooses to block on `sluice wait` while a driver moves the queue; `sluice run` by hand, from cron, by a fleet's service or by a plugin's lease, the fleet's choice and never sluice's ask; the gate box's `sluice run` as the one that runs a branch's gate under its policy; the Claude Code board carrying sluice's delivery into a session no multiplexer reaches, by `sluice inbox`, which marks delivered; a record holding the former field `told`, read as `delivered`; a toast to the host's user, which is no delivery. Also rules out: a tick that lists a branch's requests on the forge between discoveries while nothing on the host moved (installation 171576254, 2026-10-28: every three-second pass of every `sluice run`, wait and step listing each branch's open requests). Not ruled out: the discovery at the deployment's interval, conditional, which is the one question only the forge can answer (136); the remote's one fetch a tick carrying every ref the host reads from it, the records, each branch at its revision, the heads submitted to it and its combinations, one call where several would be several processes, so a branch on a remote reads as that fetch last brought it and a forecast forms from the reader's document (64, 129 as amended 2026-10-30, on batch 46's question). Source: clause 129 (as amended 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-30); README (No daemon); clauses 50, 51, 129, 135, 136; ADRs 0011, 0023, 0041, 0056, 0068, 0070, 0071.
- **core.32** Rules out: a `GET` to the forge sent without the ETag or last-modified time the host keeps for its address; an answer kept on the host used without the forge's 304 for it; a request's reviews, checks, timeline or comments read while its head, its `updated_at` and its entry's state stand and it waits on nothing; a draft's anything beyond the list; sluice's check runs read back on every pass to compare (installation 171576254, 2026-10-28: `show`'s `posted_all`, `missing`'s reviews and checks, `is_held`'s timeline per request every three seconds); a posting made that says what the last said; a token written to the host's state to share its ETags (forge-identity); the discovery interval in a repository's policy. Not ruled out: a request out of draft waiting on another system's check read again at each discovery, conditionally, since such a check moves no request's `updated_at`; GitHub answering 200 to an ETag another token received, which replaces what is kept; the REST list kept as the conditional question beside the one GraphQL query; the reads per request where the forge answers no such query or refused it. Source: clauses 128, 136 (added 2026-10-28); the C.5 pull request row; ADRs 0044, 0071.
- **core.33** Rules out: intake on a host that neither keeps the branch nor holds its intake mark where one stands; two hosts' queues holding entries of one request; the mark taken from a live driver before the lease passed by the taker's own observation, or by comparing the hosts' clocks; a wait whose request another host takes in running intake or a round for it, or reading its request on the forge more often than once in a quarter of the interval (deneb, 2026-10-28: waits running intake on branches mac-studio's `sluice run` drives); a mark pushed without a lease. Not ruled out: a host's own entries, submitted from its own places, advanced by its own rounds whoever holds the mark; a step or a wait taking the requests in where no mark stands, the host there alone, and a branch one host reaches by waits and steps alone keeping no mark between them (the user, 2026-10-29); a driver taking the mark from a wait at once; a wait on a host where no driver lives taking the mark where none is held, and letting it go when it ends; two takers both taking a request in for the one pass before one's fetch reads the other's mark; on one host, the wait that follows its host's driver (135). Source: clauses 58, 72, 73, 135, 137 (added 2026-10-28, amended 2026-10-29); C.6; ADRs 0056, 0068, 0071.
