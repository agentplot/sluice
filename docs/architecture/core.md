# sluice rules

The rules that bind wherever a change is made in this repository,
written so a reviewer can point at a diff and say which rule it breaks.
The narrative is the statement in agentplot/blueprints,
`design/sluice/requirements.md`; a rule here names the clause it
enforces and never restates it. There are no area pages yet; a rule
that binds only one part of the tree moves to an area page when that
part exists.

## Rules

### The core and its seams

- **[core.1]** Code outside a profile names no task runner, worktree tool, multiplexer, service manager or forge.
- **[core.2]** Every seam of Part B is reached through exactly one interface, and a profile implements that interface and nothing of the core.
- **[core.3]** A seam with a single profile has an ADR saying why one is enough.

### Branches and landings

- **[core.4]** No code path reachable from the submit operation writes a branch's ref; a round of sluice's work may be run by any process, a waiting driver included.
- **[core.5]** A branch is moved only with the expected old revision, and a move whose expectation fails is reported, never retried blind.
- **[core.6]** A candidate is formed from the branch's revision and the entries ahead of it, in order, and from nothing else.
- **[core.17]** A candidate whose entries ahead land exactly as they were tested is the same candidate on the revision they land as: its tree, its runs and its verdict stand, and it is named as that revision extended by the entries still ahead of it.
- **[core.27]** Which remote a branch is on is read from the policy at every revision of the branch the host holds, its own ref and each remote's tracking ref, never from the host's own ref alone and never by a fetch, and from each revision only as far as that reading needs, the remote named for the branch and whether signers are named, so a revision this sluice refuses elsewhere in its policy decides the remote all the same.
- **[core.28]** A read of a remote the core could not make is logged on the branch with the remote's own words, once per distinct cause and host, with its end, and is never reported only to the process that met it.
- **[core.29]** Every call to a remote runs under sluice's one retry policy, a maintained crate's backoff bounded by its caller, which tries again what the remote never judged, a credential it refused bought afresh for the next try, never what the remote answered, and never a push or a forge write whose answer was not read, which is settled by reading the branch or the forge, a write nothing reads back reported unanswered.
- **[core.31]** Nothing of sluice's need run between its commands for any guarantee to hold, and no policy, configuration or document of sluice's tells a host to run a daemon; where a host runs `sluice run`, it is the host's reader and a wait or a watch that finds it live runs no round; a submission's outcome is pushed into its submitter's session and recorded as told by sluice alone, on the word of the carrier that delivered it and never on a notify that delivered nothing, no carrier keeping a told list of its own; and a session that starts asks by one command for every untold outcome of its own submissions, so no wait need stand for an outcome to arrive.

### Verdicts and attribution

- **[core.7]** Every check run is recorded with its key, outcome and executor before any verdict is read from it, a fail exactly as a pass.
- **[core.8]** A failure is attributed to one entry, and only after every candidate ahead of it has a verdict.
- **[core.9]** A replay is recorded as a replay, never as a run.
- **[core.22]** A result counts for a gate only through a signer the branch's policy names and a claim that signer may make, never through what a record says of its executor alone; on a branch in one host's own files, through the runs that host's sluice starts.

### Places

- **[core.10]** The core starts no service; the checks it runs are one process group, ended with their place or their time limit, and what they hand to another process is removed when their place is released.
- **[core.11]** Every record a reader may need is readable with no sluice process running, from every host that submits to its branch.
- **[core.14]** Nothing the core decides is held between steps outside the records, and no two steps act on one branch at once.
- **[core.15]** A branch's gate is read from the branch's current revision, never from a candidate or a place.
- **[core.16]** The core releases only places it made, and a session's only when its working copy is gone; otherwise a session's place is released only by `sluice release` at its session's or driver's asking, and a preview only by `sluice release` at its caller's asking or when its command failed or its checkout is gone.
- **[core.18]** A session run counts toward the bound from the host's state and is recorded under its keys only when its worktree holds exactly its head.
- **[core.19]** A preview is made only at a revision its branch has landed, and decides nothing: its command is never recorded as a run, read by a gate, or waited on by a submission.
- **[core.20]** The core writes no working copy it neither made nor adopted: a worktree that has checked out a branch sluice lands on is reported, never moved, and never stops a landing.
- **[core.23]** An instance claims no run while a place it made belongs to a candidate with a verdict, an obsolete one, or a claim it no longer holds, until each such place is released or held.
- **[core.30]** A place's scratch is at a path that does not grow with the place's name or its checkout's location, and the place's record names it.

### Design and records

- **[core.12]** A change that bears on a clause cites the clause by number; a clause that no longer holds is changed in the statement first, never worked around here.
- **[core.13]** Nothing commercial, no pricing, tier or customer, appears in this repository.
- **[core.26]** The site, the README, the plugins' skills and the commands' output speak to any host's operator: no word the statement coined that the reader cannot see in the product, and no machine, fleet, partition, deployment or tool of ours.
- **[core.25]** On a host, at most one process reads a repository's records to watch its queues; every other watch and wait follows that reader's document.

### The gate

- **[core.21]** Every file the tree tracks is owned by a formatter the gate runs, or is excluded by name with its reason; every language with a linter is linted; every directory under `plugins/`, and `site/`, is a moon project of its own, listed in `.moon/workspace.yml` with its language declared, whose check-fast tasks validate, type-check and test it, one task per check.
- **[core.24]** Every tool a gate task runs is the version the tree pins, on every host that runs the gate, so CI and `sluice exec` hold a tree to the same rules.

## Details

- **core.1** Rules out: the strings `moon`, `wt`, `worktrunk`, `devenv`, `herdr`, `gh`, `crew`, `flywheel` in any module outside a profile directory; an import of a profile from the core. Not ruled out: `git`, which section 9 of the statement fixes; `plugins/`, which no crate imports. Source: statement preamble and clause 52; ADRs 0003, 0023.
- **core.2** Rules out: a profile calling another profile; a core function with a profile-specific branch. Source: clauses 52, 53; ADR 0003.
- **core.3** Rules out: a seam whose only profile is undocumented as such. Source: ADR 0003.
- **core.4** Rules out: `update-ref`, `push`, a merge or a fast-forward in the submit operation or anything it calls; a round that behaves differently because a driver ran it; a submit pushing, or writing the host's copy of, a branch sluice lands on (124). Not ruled out: a waiting `sluice submit`, `wt submit` or `sluice wait` running rounds between reads of its submission's state; a pull request's submit pushing the session's own branch, which sluice lands on nowhere (124). Source: clauses 1, 124; ADRs 0002, 0005, 0011, 0041, 0053.
- **core.5** Rules out: `update-ref` without an old value; a landing loop that re-reads and retries on refusal without re-forming the chain. Not ruled out: a leased push the remote refused on sluice's credential before any ref was negotiated, tried again under the retry policy on a credential bought afresh, with the lease and the expectation of the first, since the remote it refused is as it was (134; record 0062). Source: clauses 24, 45, 46, 134.
- **core.6** Rules out: a candidate formed from a cached revision of the branch; a candidate including an entry removed as culprit or conflict, or an entry behind it; a candidate formed on a revision that lacks the branch's last landing (121). Source: clauses 8, 10, 12, 21, 121; ADR 0051.
- **core.17** Rules out: testing a candidate again because the entries ahead of it landed as tested; telling a culprit, or recording, a combination by a revision the branch has left when the branch has since landed the entries ahead of it. Holds because a landing commit carries the tested candidate's tree (23): the branch after the landing and the candidate before it hold the same tree. Source: clauses 10, 20, 23; answers to the reconstruction, 12; ADR 0006.
- **core.27** Rules out: a routing that reads a branch as local because the host's own ref holds no policy, or one naming no remote, while a remote's tracking ref of the branch names one (deneb, 2026-10-26: agentplot-kit's and site-theme's `refs/heads/main` from before the policy file, `sluice branch create` refusing crew's bolt, signal 2026-10-26-madswan-planner-ec418792/01); a fetch of any remote run to decide the routing; a branch routed to a remote no revision names; two revisions naming different remotes resolved by order rather than refused, naming both; a routing refused by a table or field of a revision's policy that names neither the remote nor a signer (deneb, 2026-10-28: `[[checkout.cache]]` at 4f6fe55f on the host's own ref of bolt/a-run-compiles-through-a-shared-cache refusing every read of the branch after the user's clean push to its remote, signal 2026-10-28-slc-2-conductor-3f0e396d/01; record 0069). Not ruled out: the fetch the pushed refs profile makes once the branch is routed to its remote (C.7); a host that holds no revision of the branch reading it as absent; the policy read whole at the branch's revision on its remote, for its gate (56). Source: clause 124 (as amended 2026-10-28); ADRs 0060, 0069.
- **core.28** Rules out: a round that fails on a refused fetch of the branch or its records with nothing written to the branch's log once it can write; a wait's `could not read` line as the one record of a standing refusal (deneb, 2026-10-26: swancloud-ops read `main/log.jsonl` for half an hour while every round's fetch was refused and found no fetch error, signal 2026-10-26-madswan-planner-c13058ca/01); a line per refused round; the remote's words shortened or rewritten; a refusal logged with no host. Not ruled out: the refusal's first time, words and count kept in the host's own state until the records on the remote can be written (73); the chore's second read on a fresh credential before the refusal is read as standing. Source: clause 133; ADR 0061.
- **core.29** Rules out: a sleep-and-try-again loop written at a call site (the wait's run through 5xx and the chore's second read, once the policy is in); a backoff, jitter or retry predicate of sluice's own in place of the crate's; a call to a remote, by git or by HTTP, made outside the policy; a push `[rejected]` under its lease, a protected branch or a 4xx that is the answer tried again; a push whose answer was not read tried again; a check run created, a comment posted or a workflow dispatched again after a server error without the forge read first; a workflow dispatch sent twice; a second try on the credential just refused; a retry logged on the branch per try. Not ruled out: the policy bounded to a wait's five minutes and to seconds for a round or a command; a fresh token settled before it is handed over; a landing left unfinished settled by the next step from its intent (122, `perform_landing`); `release_retries`, a release run once a round under the branch's policy; a request's draft mark set again, since the state read decides; a check run or a comment the forge already holds carried on; a dispatch whose connection was never made sent again; the crate's choice within the class of maintained backoff crates (backon recommended, 2026-10-27). Source: clauses 45, 46, 133, 134 (as amended 2026-10-27 for forge writes, on batch 35's question); ADRs 0017, 0061, 0062.
- **core.31** Rules out: a README, guide, skill or setup step that tells an adopter to run or install a service for sluice to work; a policy word or a host configuration key that requires a driver; a `sluice wait`, a waiting submit or a watch running rounds on a host where `sluice run` lives; an outcome told only by the wait that happened to read it; an outcome whose delivery is recorded nowhere; an entry marked told by a notify that delivered nothing (the worktrunk driver on main, 2026-10-28); a plugin's own list of outcomes told, or a plugin that marks sluice's records except through a sluice command; two carriers telling one outcome to one session; a session restarted after its submission was decided left to find out by asking status; a reader's tick, a watch's or `sluice run`'s, that starts a process or reads an object while nothing in the repository changed (mac-studio, 2026-10-28, sluice 0.51.3: an idle `sluice run` in flywheel-web started git about 1364 times and made about 54,000 file events in ten minutes with nothing queued, reading each branch's policy through git every three-second pass, swancloud-ops's reading); a branch's policy read again while its revision stood. Not ruled out: a wait that runs rounds on a host where nothing else does, as today and for every plain install; a session that chooses to block on `sluice wait` while a driver moves the queue; `sluice run` by hand, from cron, by a fleet's service or by a plugin's lease, the fleet's choice and never sluice's ask; the gate box's `sluice run` as the one that runs a branch's gate under its policy; the Claude Code board carrying sluice's telling into a session no multiplexer reaches, by sluice's one command, which marks told; a toast to the host's user, which is no delivery. Source: clause 129 (as amended 2026-10-28); README (No daemon); clauses 50, 51, 129, 135; ADRs 0011, 0023, 0041, 0056, 0068.
- **core.30** Rules out: a scratch path that carries the place's name, the worktree's path or any escaped form of either; a scratch path derived from the place's name when a release needs it rather than read from the record; a release that leaves a scratch no record names under `sluice/.tmp/`; a scratch removed because its place's record exists and could not be read, which names its scratch still (35): the record is reported with the reader's words and nothing of that place is removed. Not ruled out: the scratch under the repository's own `sluice/.tmp/`; a place made before this record, whose record names no scratch, released at the path the former scheme gave it. Source: clauses 34, 35 (as amended 2026-10-27 for an unreadable record), 123; ADR 0063.
- **core.7** Rules out: reading a verdict from the task runner's cache alone; discarding a failed run's record; writing a record after the verdict; removing a result's line because it was set aside. Not ruled out: a run's tasks kept while it goes, and when its output grew (ADRs 0028, 0042), which are no record of a run and which no verdict reads; a check a run did not judge, which has no outcome and is recorded under no key (60; could-not-run-1 114); a check the time limit ended, which has none either, whatever the runner reported of it (time-limit-1 66); a result the operator set aside, whose line stays and which no verdict reads (could-not-run-1 116). Source: clauses 16, 18, 60, 66; could-not-run-1; time-limit-1; ADRs 0004, 0028, 0031, 0042, 0043.
- **core.8** Rules out: attributing from a batch with no intermediate verdicts; attributing while a candidate ahead is still running; telling two sessions of one failure. Source: clauses 17, 18, 20.
- **core.9** Rules out: counting a cache hit as an executor's observation. Source: clause 16, 38; ADR 0004.
- **core.10** Rules out: starting an emulator, a server or a process manager from the core; a check's process group left running after its run or its place; releasing a place without running its release command. Not ruled out: the place seam giving a place its ports; a session's run, in its own worktree, under the executor (session-runs-1 clause 89); a preview's command handing off a stack that stays until the preview is released (previews-1 clause 98); a place whose working copy is gone, its stored release run once in its scratch directory and the place freed whatever it did; and a place freed at the operator's accepting that its release failed, with nothing run in its name (123). Source: clauses 31 to 34, 66, 123; previews-1 clause 98; ADRs 0010, 0016, 0018, 0045, 0055.
- **core.11** Rules out: state held only in a running process's memory; a database only a server reads; records reached other than through the records seam; on a branch whose records are on its remote, a state that only one host's files hold; a submission's record, wait or status read refused because another branch's policy or records could not be read (deneb, 2026-10-28: `sluice wait 250` exited 3 at once on bolt/a-run-compiles-through-a-shared-cache's policy, signal 2026-10-28-slc-2-conductor-3f0e396d/01). Not ruled out: a run's tasks, kept through the records seam beside the branch's records on the host that runs it and read there alone, since they decide nothing (ADR 0028); a session run's tasks and command, kept by its own `sluice exec` in the host's state beside its count and read there through the check seam, since a session run is no branch's (session-runs-1 C.3; ADR 0035). Source: clause 26 (as amended 2026-10-28); clauses 26, 51, 61 to 63, 80; C.6; ADRs 0008, 0028, 0035, 0037.
- **core.14** Rules out: a decision kept in a loop's memory and not in the records; a step that proceeds without the branch's lock; behavior that differs between `sluice run` and `sluice step`; a run of every branch's rounds ended by one branch it could not read, where a step of any other branch runs (deneb, 2026-10-28: `sluice run` ended on bolt/a-run-compiles-through-a-shared-cache's refused policy, signal 2026-10-28-slc-2-conductor-3f0e396d/01). Source: clauses 26 (as amended 2026-10-28), 51, S10; ADR 0005.
- **core.15** Rules out: reading policy from a candidate's tree or a session's place; a submission whose change to the policy relaxes its own gate. Not ruled out: the overview's order of branches, read from the default branch's policy, which no round, gate, landing or submission reads (ADR 0036); the task runner's own setting of which tasks it keeps out of CI, read from the submitting worktree at submit, which no gate counts (ADR 0050). Source: clauses 27, 56; ADRs 0007, 0036, 0050.
- **core.16** Rules out: removing or cleaning a session's worktree on landing; a round or a run releasing a session's place whose worktree exists, running its release command or letting its ports go, however long since its last run; a candidate place left behind after its verdict is read. Not ruled out: `sluice release`, `sluice exec --release` and a driver's removal releasing a session's place; `sluice release --accept-failure` freeing a session's place at its session's or driver's asking, the worktree left (123); the next round or `sluice exec` on the host freeing a session's place whose worktree no longer exists, which is no session's, once its stored release has run once from its scratch directory, no policy read and no toolchain entered (123); a round, `sluice exec`, `sluice preview` or `sluice previews` releasing a preview whose command failed, or freeing one whose checkout is gone once its stored release has run the same way (123). Also rules out: a round, a step or a landing releasing a preview that is up, starting or waiting with its checkout in place. Source: clauses 25, 34, 123; session-runs-1 clauses 95, 97; previews-1 clause 101; ADRs 0018, 0020, 0045, 0055.
- **core.18** Rules out: a session run started when the count, read under the host's lock, leaves no room outside the reserve; a session run's count held in a process's memory or in a branch's records; recording a run from a worktree that holds anything beyond its head, a command's run, or a replay as a run. Not ruled out: a session run's tasks and command kept on its record in the host's state while it runs, and its result's tasks and whether its gate takes it, none of which a round, gate or replay reads (ADRs 0035, 0027). Source: clauses 16, 71; session-runs-1 clauses 89, 91, 92; ADRs 0012, 0018, 0035.
- **core.19** Rules out: a preview of a revision the branch has not landed, a combination's or a session's head; a preview's run written to `results.jsonl`; a gate, a round or a submission reading a preview's state; the gate's executor or limits read from the previewed revision rather than the branch's current one. Not ruled out: the preview's command, ports and release command read from the previewed revision, whose stack they describe. Source: previews-1 clauses 98, 103; clauses 27, 56; ADR 0045.
- **core.20** Rules out: a `read-tree`, `reset`, `checkout`, `switch` or rebase of a worktree that is no place of sluice's, after a landing or at any other time; a landing, a round or a submission refused or held because a worktree has its branch checked out. Not ruled out: moving the branch itself under such a worktree, which a landing does (45); naming the worktree in `sluice status`, `sluice overview` and `sluice protect`; a round levelling the host's copy under such a worktree (124). Source: clauses 1, 25, 124; ADRs 0018, 0024, 0053.
- **core.22** Rules out: counting a run whose record no signer the policy names has signed, on a branch whose records are on its remote; reading a run as confined, or as on a system, when its signer may not claim it; counting a replay with no trusted signer's pass for its key on its system; landing on a verdict the state holds without reading the signed runs again. Not ruled out: a one-host branch's unsigned runs, which its own sluice started. Source: clauses 27 to 29, 69, 78, 79, 108; ADRs 0032, 0033.
- **core.23** Rules out: a claim taken by a host with a candidate place left from a verdict, an obsolete candidate or a lapsed claim, before that place's release has been tried; a result reported under a claim its reporter no longer holds. Not ruled out: a place on a host that never returns, which is the operator's to remove; a claim taken while a place the host made is held, named with what failed (81, 123). Source: clauses 25, 32, 34, 77, 81, 123; ADRs 0037, 0055.
- **core.12** Rules out: a comment or commit that reinterprets a clause; a behavior the statement forbids kept behind a flag. Source: ADR 0001.
- **core.13** Rules out: a pricing table, a tier name, a customer name, a hosted-plan reference in any file. Source: ADR 0001.
- **core.21** Rules out: a file type treefmt neither formats nor excludes; an exclude with no comment saying why; a plugin directory or the site with no project of its own in `.moon/workspace.yml`, or one with no language declared; a part's check run as a task of another project, the root's included; one task bundling several checks; a plugin directory whose manifest, types or tests no task of its project runs; a task in a change whose only verification is a command run by hand, unless the task says why the gate cannot run it. Not ruled out: a check that spans the parts, the marketplace's, as a task of the root; the formatters and linters treefmt runs over the whole tree in `root:format`; the site's build, which is the Pages workflow's; the site's prose check, `site:prose`, as its one check, since the site has no tests and its type check waits until a place holds the site's dependencies; `*.md` outside the site, which no gate reads. Source: ADRs 0029, 0030.
- **core.24** Rules out: a tool a task finds outside its pin, in rustup's default toolchain or a runner's own install; a pinned toolchain without a component a task runs; two files pinning one tool with no check that fails when they differ. Not ruled out: the hosts' platforms differing, macOS's gate and CI's Linux, which pins can't make one; a test that leans on one platform's behaviour, ports a host hands out among it, fails on the other. Source: ADR 0040.
- **core.25** Rules out: a watch that reads the records while a live reader's document exists on the host; a wait that polls the records beside a reader; a reader serving another host. Not ruled out: a one-shot `sluice overview` reading the records itself; a follower taking the reader's place when its lease lapses. Source: clause 129; ADR 0056.
- **core.26** Rules out: "the keeper", "the fleet", "the partition", a host or team of ours, or swancloud, crew and herdr as the reader's own, on a page, in a skill or in output; "change it in swancloud's machine configuration" where "change it where the host's configuration is managed" is meant. Not ruled out: naming a tool a profile integrates with (moon, worktrunk, GitHub, herdr as a product); the statement and the records keeping their own vocabulary. Source: ADR 0058; the user, 2026-10-26.
