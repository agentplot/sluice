## 2. The seam

- [x] 3.2 `decide`, once the branch is not held, reads `copy` and, for `At(c)` with `c != Some(landed)`, levels it from `c` to `landed`; on failure or `Elsewhere` it writes this host's `copies` entry (keyed by `peer.name`) in the round's write and logs `copy-behind` once per distinct `(at, lacks, why)`; on the copy at `landed`, or levelled, it removes the entry and logs `copy-levelled` when there was one; a held branch levels nothing; verify conformance cases over the fakes: a copy two landings behind levelled by the next round; a hand's commit on top of the landing leaves the copy at the landing; three failing rounds write one `copy-behind` line; a held branch's copy unchanged, then levelled to the accepted revision after `accept_branch`; a local-refs branch never levelled.
