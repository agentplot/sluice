name, or one test of `--test cli` over the fakes, is quick and runs

reads or writes the host's own settings: each names its own file, or
sets `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` to a scratch directory, as
`crates/sluice/tests/instance.rs` does.

tree touched. Cite clause 135, record 0107 and rule core.54, and the
design's decisions by D-number. Name no task runner in
